## Open conflict (hand-off to systems-engineer)

`TSR-001` requires SAFE_OFF to de-energise the LED driver stages. For the low beam this is in
direct conflict with the safe state of `SG-01`, which is limp-home operation with the remaining
channel visible, not darkness. Asserting SAFE_OFF on the low-beam channels therefore *creates* the
hazard H-01 that `SG-01` exists to prevent. The hardware view is that the disable path must be
differentiated by channel class (work lamps and high beam off, low beam into a hardware default
state), but that is a technical-safety-concept decision and is left to `systems-engineer`.
