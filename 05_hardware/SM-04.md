---
id: SM-04
text: >
  Overcurrent protection in the LED driver stage: hardware current limit at the output stage,
  latch-off on a persistent limit condition, and reporting of the event through DriverStatus to the
  microcontroller.
type: safety-mechanism
asil: B
source: FSR-002
derived_from: [FSR-002, TSR-002]
allocated_to: [LED_Driver_Stage_1]
verified_by: []
status: draft
detected_fault: >
  Short of a channel output to ground, short across part of an LED string, and overload of the
  output stage. Not covered: overcurrent in the harness upstream of the shunt.
detection_time: >
  Current limit engages within 10 us (analogue loop); latch-off within 5 ms; status readable within
  10 ms (HW-REQ-021, HW-REQ-007).
reaction_time: >
  <= 150 ms - fault reaction of TSR-004 once the status has been read.
ftti_budget: >
  15 ms detection + 150 ms reaction = 165 ms against the 300 ms FTTI of SG-01. Margin 135 ms (45 %).
  The component-protection part of the mechanism is analogue and is two orders of magnitude faster
  than the FTTI; only the reporting path is in the FTTI budget.
diagnostic_coverage: >
  Target 60 % of the output-stage overcurrent failure modes (plausible example value, not
  validated). Claim NOT confirmed - owned by safety-analyst via the FMEDA. The limiting factor is
  that the mechanism protects rather than diagnoses: a latched channel is a lost channel, and it is
  the status readback that turns it into a detected fault.
owning_hw_req: [HW-REQ-007, HW-REQ-021]
rationale: >
  Without the status readback the latch-off is indistinguishable from an open load at the shunt, and
  the fault reaction would be correct by accident rather than by evidence.
---
