text: >
  Short-to-battery detection on a low-beam channel by evaluating the channel output voltage during
  the PWM off-phase against a threshold, debounced, and used to discriminate a short to battery
  from an open load before the fault reaction is triggered.
type: safety-mechanism
asil: B
source: SYS-REQ-019
derived_from: [SYS-REQ-019, TSR-003]
allocated_to: [LED_Driver_Stage_1, ECU_LightingCtrl]
verified_by: []
status: draft
detected_fault: >
  External short of a channel output to battery potential; also detects a channel output stuck in
  the conducting state. Not covered: a short to battery behind the LED module connector that keeps
  the string current in the normal band.
detection_time: >
  <= 45 ms = 10 ms sampling interval of U_Channel + 30 ms qualification (HW-REQ-020) + 5 ms task
  latency.
reaction_time: >
  <= 150 ms - fault reaction of TSR-004 (limp-home, DTC, driver warning request).
ftti_budget: >
  45 ms detection + 150 ms reaction = 195 ms against the 300 ms FTTI of SG-01. Margin 105 ms (35 %).
diagnostic_coverage: >
  Contributes to the SM-01 coverage claim rather than carrying an independent one; the hardware-side
  split is in analysis_sm01_coverage.md. Number owned by safety-analyst via the FMEDA.
owning_hw_req: [HW-REQ-006, HW-REQ-020]
rationale: >
  Without this mechanism a short to battery is read at the shunt as a current collapse and is
  misclassified as an open load, which is a wrong fault reaction on a channel that is in fact
  conducting. See analysis_current_sensing.md section 5.
