---
id: SM-06
text: >
  Supply voltage monitoring: window comparators on the protected battery voltage in the
  Power_Supply_Unit with a reported condition and a hardware overvoltage shutdown of the driver
  stages, plus independent monitoring of the internal rails by the ASIC_Watchdog with reset and
  SAFE_OFF.
type: safety-mechanism
asil: B
source: FSR-002
derived_from: [FSR-002, TSR-001, SYS-REQ-012]
allocated_to: [Power_Supply_Unit, ASIC_Watchdog, LED_Driver_Stage_1]
verified_by: []
status: draft
detected_fault: >
  Undervoltage and overvoltage at the vehicle supply, load dump beyond the withstand level of
  HW-REQ-013, and drift or loss of an internal supply rail including the ADC reference. Not covered:
  a rail that fails inside its tolerance band, which is why HW-REQ-010 adds an independent reference
  plausibility check for the measurement path.
detection_time: >
  <= 10 ms for the battery-voltage window (HW-REQ-016), <= 5 ms for an internal rail (HW-REQ-017).
reaction_time: >
  <= 1 ms hardware shutdown above 40 V (HW-REQ-016); <= 10 ms via the enable gate for the rail
  branch (HW-REQ-019).
ftti_budget: >
  10 ms detection + 10 ms reaction = 20 ms against the 300 ms FTTI of SG-01. Margin 280 ms (93 %).
diagnostic_coverage: >
  Target 90 % of the supply failure modes of the ECU (plausible example value, not validated).
  Claim NOT confirmed - owned by safety-analyst via the FMEDA. The hardware-side condition is that
  the comparator thresholds themselves are testable at power-up, otherwise a stuck comparator is a
  latent fault of the mechanism.
owning_hw_req: [HW-REQ-011, HW-REQ-012, HW-REQ-016, HW-REQ-017]
rationale: >
  Both the open-load threshold of SM-01 and the constant-current regulation of the driver stage rest
  on rails that are within tolerance; an undetected reference drift shifts the 150 mA threshold with
  no other symptom. Voltage monitoring is therefore a precondition of SM-01, not an independent
  mechanism.
