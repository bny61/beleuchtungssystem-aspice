id: SM-05
text: >
  Overtemperature protection of the LED modules by NTC measurement with plausibility band and a
  linear derating curve that reduces the channel set point between 105 C and 125 C down to the
  400 mA derating floor, with a diagnostic trouble code once derating is active.
type: safety-mechanism
asil: B
source: CR-014
derived_from: [CR-014, FSR-002]
allocated_to: [Temp_Sense_Chain, LED_Driver_Stage_1]
verified_by: []
status: draft
detected_fault: >
  Thermal overload of an LED module (blocked convection, soiling, elevated cavity temperature,
  degraded thermal interface) and failure of the temperature sensing itself (NTC open or shorted,
  detected by the plausibility band of HW-REQ-022). Not covered: a thermal fault local to a single
  LED die, which the module NTC does not see.
detection_time: >
  <= 305 ms = 100 ms sampling interval + 3-sample qualification + 5 ms task latency
  (HW-REQ-022).
reaction_time: >
  <= 1 s for the set-point ramp to the derated value; the ramp is deliberately slow so that
  derating is not visible as a luminous step.
ftti_budget: >
  0.3 s detection + 1 s reaction = 1.3 s against a thermal FTTI of at least 10 s (A-21, the module
  thermal time constant is about 60 s). Margin >= 87 %. Note that the SG-01 FTTI of 300 ms does not
  apply here: the hazard is degradation, not the sudden loss of the low beam.
diagnostic_coverage: >
  Target 70 % of the thermal-overload failure modes at module level, of which the NTC sensor faults
  are covered by the plausibility band (plausible example values, not validated). Claim NOT
  confirmed - owned by safety-analyst via the FMEDA.
owning_hw_req: [HW-REQ-008, HW-REQ-022, HW-REQ-023, HW-REQ-024]
rationale: >
  The derating floor makes this mechanism a safety mechanism rather than a pure protection function:
  it must reduce power without ever taking the low beam below the level at which SM-01 can still
  distinguish a real open load from commanded derating (HW-REQ-002, HW-REQ-008).
