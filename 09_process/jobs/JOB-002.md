## Decisions (from the human, binding for this run)

1. **Five CRs**, `CR-024 … CR-028`, as listed above.

2. **Do not set an ASIL.** Every new record gets `asil: QM`. Determining the ASIL of a customer
   requirement is the safety-manager's call and follows from the HARA, not from the systems
   engineer's judgement — `OP-1` had to correct exactly that once already. State in each
   `rationale` that the ASIL is open pending safety-manager assignment, and raise one open point
   covering all five.

3. **`CR-026` must not contradict the `SG-01` safe state.** The limp-home state operates the
   remaining channel, which `analysis_thermal_derating.md` puts near 19 % of nominal flux at the
   400 mA floor. A requirement demanding a fixed illuminance after a channel failure would make
   the safe state a violation of the customer specification. So: word `CR-026` against the
   *legal minimum* for a degraded low beam rather than against the nominal level, and if a
   number is needed, derive it from the analysis and mark it as requiring confirmation. Do not
   invent a value that the safe state cannot meet.

**If any of this turns out to be unworkable, stop and say so rather than working around it.**
