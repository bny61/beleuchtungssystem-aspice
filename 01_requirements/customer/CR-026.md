## Context

Customer requirement from phase 1 (SYS.1), added in a later SYS.1 increment (illuminance topic).

Complements `CR-008` (limp-home behaviour: keep operating the remaining channel, store the fault)
with the photometric side of the same state: how much light the degraded system still has to put
on the road.

**Deliberately not quantified.** The candidate value would follow from `A-13` and the load-line
analysis in `05_hardware/analysis_thermal_derating.md` — a channel held at the 400 mA derating
floor produces roughly 232 lm, about 19 % of nominal flux. Whether that meets the legal passing-beam
minimum is exactly the question of `OP-38` and is not decided here. Any illuminance number entered
into this record before `OP-38` is closed requires confirmation against the photometric
measurement, and would, if set too high, contradict the safe state of `SG-01`.

Note also that the same 400 mA operating point occurs in the undervoltage case of `SYS-REQ-013`,
so the answer to `OP-38` applies to more than the channel-failure case.
