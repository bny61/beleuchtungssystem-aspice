## Context

Customer requirement from phase 1 (SYS.1), added in a later SYS.1 increment (illuminance topic).

Content-wise this record belongs to the `SG-02` thread (no unintended glare). It bounds the
static glare of the low beam; the dynamic, object-dependent glare-free high beam is covered by
`CR-003`.

The exact regulatory value and the clause it comes from are **not** cited here — the number is a
project example value and must be replaced by the homologation figure before any baseline.
