## Context

Customer requirement from phase 1 (SYS.1), added in a later SYS.1 increment (illuminance topic).

The temperature range is the one already agreed in `CR-014`; the normal supply range is the one of
`CR-016` / `CR-017` (below 9 V the system enters the undervoltage behaviour of `SYS-REQ-013`, which
is likewise out of scope of this record).

Two intentional carve-outs, both because the system is designed to leave the band on purpose:
thermal derating (`HW-REQ-023`) and undervoltage operation (`SYS-REQ-013`). Whether further
carve-outs are needed (e.g. the LED warm-up phase after switch-on) is open and should be settled
with the OEM before this record leaves `draft`.
