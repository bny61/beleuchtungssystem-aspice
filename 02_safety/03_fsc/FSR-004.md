  When the lighting system has detected the failure of a low-beam channel, it shall warn the
  driver visually within 2 s.

  are deliberately longer than the 300 ms FTTI - see the phase 2 timing budget.

## Context

The warning is the measure that underpins the C rating of the HARA; it does not replace the
technical fault reaction.
