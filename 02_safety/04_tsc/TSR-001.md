text: >
  The lighting ECU shall monitor its own program execution by an external watchdog with an independent time base and shall de-energise the LED driver stages into the defined off state if the watchdog is not served within 50 ms.
type: safety
asil: B
source: FSR-002
derived_from: [FSR-002]
allocated_to: [ECU_LightingCtrl, ASIC_Watchdog]
verified_by: []
status: draft
rationale: >
  Covers loss of control of the microcontroller, which would otherwise allow unintended activation or deactivation of any channel. Independent time base is required so that a clock fault cannot disable the monitor. Plausible example value.
